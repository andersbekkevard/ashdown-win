# 0005 — ashdown.win, an identity that belongs to the house

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The poster by the table must carry a URL a person can type from memory.
The site should feel like it belongs to Ashdown House rather than to the
person who built it, so that residents adopt it and a future resident can
inherit it. A pool ladder or another game may follow.

## Options considered

- Domains naming the sport: ashdownpong.com, ashdowntt.com, ashdownelo.com.
  Clear, but tie the site to table tennis.
- ashdown.house: names the building exactly. Judged too official in tone.
- ashdown.gg: gamer-coded and short, but premium priced at roughly 130 dollars
  a year.
- ashdown.tt: table tennis in the suffix, several hundred dollars a year.
- ashdown.win (selected): about 27 dollars a year, self-explanatory, and every
  player URL reads as a claim.

## Decision

The domain is ashdown.win. The repository is ashdown-win. Both name the
house, not the sport or the author.

## Consequences

- The poster, the URL, and the repository share one string.
- A second game can join under the same name with its own match log.
- Annual cost is low enough that a future resident can take over the
  renewal without thought.

## Reversal triggers

- None foreseen.
