# Fall Guys

Loud, candy-coloured, chunky. Every surface is a thick rounded slab with a
white rim and a hard, unblurred drop shadow. Words that
matter are huge, white with a pink stroke and an ink shadow, tilted two
degrees. Rank is a hexagon badge; first place wears a gold crown. Buttons are
fat pills that squash on press. Backgrounds are never flat: lemon with a faint
crown pattern in print, sky blue with concentric rings on screen. Confetti and
stars appear only on a result, never on a form.

## Palette (sampled from screenshots)

Bubblegum #FB3AA3 (primary, banners, selected). Raspberry #C81C74 (stroke,
pressed). Beach Ball #00B5FE (screen background, secondary). Deep Blue #0866D7
(rings). Banana #FCC601 (poster background). Crown Gold #FFD046 (rank 1,
stars). Lime Bonbon #5EEAC0 (wins). Lilac #7C7BE8 (inactive). Ink #222126
(outlines, shadows, text). Paper #FFFFFF (cards, rims).

## Type

Titan One for headlines, celebratory numbers and the URL. Fredoka 500 to 700
for everything else. Fallback: Arial Rounded MT Bold, system-ui. Outlined type
uses `paint-order: stroke fill`.

## Shape

Base unit 8px. Cards 24px radius, buttons full pill, hexagon badges via
clip-path. Rims 4px white on colour, 3px ink on white. Shadows offset only:
0 6px 0 ink for cards, 0 5px 0 for buttons, 0 1px 0 when pressed.

## Why it suits a dorm ladder

The ladder exists to get people to the table, not to rank them precisely. A
party-game look tells a passer-by that losing costs nothing and that recording
a match is a thirty-second joke between friends, not paperwork. The crown, the
confetti and the squashing buttons reward playing; the hexagon badges and hard
shadows keep the board legible from across the lounge.
