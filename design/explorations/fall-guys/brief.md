# Fall Guys

Loud, candy-coloured, chunky. Every surface is a thick rounded slab with a
white rim and a hard, unblurred drop shadow. Words that
matter are huge, white with a pink stroke and an ink shadow, tilted two
degrees. Rank is a hexagon badge; first place wears a gold crown. Buttons are
fat pills that squash on press. Backgrounds are never flat: lemon with a faint
crown pattern in print, sky blue with concentric rings on screen. Confetti and
stars appear only on a result, never on a form.

## Palette (sampled from screenshots)

Bubblegum #FB3AA3 (primary, banners, selected). Raspberry #C81C74 (stroke,
pressed). Beach Ball #00B5FE (screen background, secondary). Deep Blue #0866D7
(rings). Banana #FCC601 (poster background). Crown Gold #FFD046 (rank 1,
stars). Lime Bonbon #5EEAC0 (wins). Lilac #7C7BE8 (inactive). Ink #222126
(outlines, shadows, text). Paper #FFFFFF (cards, rims).

## Type

Titan One for headlines, celebratory numbers and the URL. Fredoka 500 to 700
for everything else. Fallback: Arial Rounded MT Bold, system-ui. Outlined type
uses `paint-order: stroke fill`.

## Shape

Base unit 8px. Cards 24px radius, buttons full pill, hexagon badges via
clip-path. Rims 4px white on colour, 3px ink on white. Shadows offset only:
0 6px 0 ink for cards, 0 5px 0 for buttons, 0 1px 0 when pressed.

## Why it suits a dorm ladder

The ladder exists to get people to the table, not to rank them precisely. A
party-game look tells a passer-by that losing costs nothing and that recording
a match is a thirty-second joke between friends, not paperwork. The crown, the
confetti and the squashing buttons reward playing; the hexagon badges and hard
shadows keep the board legible from across the lounge.

## Refinement 2026-09-07

Applied Anders' feedback without changing the style language.

- Rating graph rebuilt for a first-time reader: the 1000 line is dashed lilac
  and captioned "everyone starts here"; each segment is mint going up and pink
  going down, with the area between the line and 1000 tinted the same way; the
  current rating sits in a gold NOW pill level with the last dot; hover or tap
  (drag works on touch) shows a chunky white tooltip with date, opponent,
  partner, delta pill and before → after. Tapping a row in the match list
  pins the same point. The list and tooltip share one timeline derived from
  `history`, so deltas agree everywhere (opponents for the first eight matches
  are mockup fill).
- Primary actions moved to a bottom dock within thumb reach: Record match and
  Create player as two fat pills on the board and player screens; on the
  record screen the dock holds the single Record it button. The top tab bar
  and the in-page button stack are gone.
- Doubles is a macOS-style round-knob switch in candy colours (grey track off,
  pink on, knob reads 1v1 / 2v2) on its own slab row with a one-line hint of
  what changes; the segmented tab is gone.
- The home screen lost its big headline: the board slab is the hero, titled
  with the outlined shout, a one-line white kicker above it, players and match
  count in the head. Rows are slightly tighter so more of the board is above
  the fold.
- The poster QR is a real code for https://ashdown.win, generated by an inline
  encoder (byte mode, EC level Q, version 2, 25 modules) verified with an
  independent decoder. It is 74 mm on A4 (about 2.6 mm per module), which
  scans from two metres on a current phone camera. The URL shrank to 37pt and
  the step cards to 40 mm to make room.
- Not re-rendered in headless Chrome after the final edit; the poster's URL
  block layout should be checked once at print size.
