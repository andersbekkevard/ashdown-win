The Ashdown table tennis ladder. One table. One board. One rating per player. No accounts.

Play a match at the table, type in who won, and the board moves. Every match ever recorded is public, so any rating can be traced back to the games that produced it.

athena% ladder --help
[1] create player Type your name once. From then on anyone can file a match with you in it. You start at 1000.
[2] record match Pick the two names, tap the side that won. Doubles is a toggle. Three taps, no login.
[3] watch the board Winner takes points from the loser. The ladder and your rating graph update at once.
athena% ladder --rule
R' = R + K·(S − E)      E = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_opp − R) / 400))
K = 32 singles, 16 doubles.  S = 1 win, 0 loss.  Everyone starts at 1000.
