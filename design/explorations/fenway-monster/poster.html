The Ashdown
Table Tennis Board

A public rating ladder for the table in the basement. No accounts, no sign-up. Anyone records a match; the winner takes points off the loser and the board changes the moment you file it.

Green lamp, you took points. Red lamp, you gave them up. Doubles is played as the average of the two partners and counts for half.

Every match ever filed is public. Filed one wrong? Delete it; the deletion goes on the board like anything else.
