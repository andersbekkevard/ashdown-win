Table TennisHouse Ladder

Every match on the board

One table, one board, one rating each. Nobody signs in and nobody is in charge: anyone can record a match between any two residents, the winner takes points from the loser, and the ladder on the wall moves before you have put the paddle down.

1

Create your name

Once, under your own name. You appear on the board at 1000 straight away.

2

Record a match

Type both names, tap the side that won. Singles or doubles. Three taps, done.

3

Watch the board

Ratings are recomputed from every match ever played, so the board is always honest.

The rule R′ = R + K (S − E),  E = 1 / (1 + 10(Ropp − R)/400) K = 32 singles · K = 16 doubles · everyone starts at 1000
