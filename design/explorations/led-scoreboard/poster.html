Ashdown House · MIT Table tennis · Elo ladder · no accounts Live

ashdown.win is the rating ladder for the table downstairs. No accounts, no app, nothing to install. Record a match from your phone and the board moves before you have put the paddle down.

Create your name

Full name by default, or your MIT username if you would rather. No password, no email. That is the whole sign-up.

Every game

Record a match

Type two names, tap the winner. Playing doubles? Flip the switch and add the partners. Three taps, done.

Always

Watch the board

Ratings update instantly. Every match ever played is public, so every number on the board can be checked.

The rule
R′ = R + K · (S − E)    where   E = 1 / (1 + 10(Ropp − R) / 400)
S is 1 for a win, 0 for a loss. K = 32 singles, 16 doubles. Everyone starts at 1000. Standard Elo, same as chess.
