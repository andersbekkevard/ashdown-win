# Official Notice

"The house has always had an official rating."

## Style language

The ladder is presented as a standing function of the residence, as if a
Committee on the Rating had posted notices since the building opened. Nothing
winks. The layout is a formal notice: seal at the head, house name in spaced
capitals, a double rule, notice number and date, numbered sections with section
marks, and a footer stating who may remove the sheet. Rules are hairlines.
Paper is off-white, ink near-black. The only colour is one green for rank one,
the way a registrar's stamp is the only colour on a form.

The crest is invented and drawn in SVG: two crossed paddles under a ball, in a
double ring reading ASHDOWN HOUSE · CAMBRIDGE above and OFFICIAL RATING ·
MCMXXXVIII below. The year is the house's founding; the seal claims the rating
is as old as the building.

The app carries the same letterhead on every screen. Actions are forms with
reference numbers (Form P-1, Form L-1). The leaderboard is the Register of
Players. Figures are tabular. Small caps replace bold.

## References

- Brown University seals, https://libguides.brown.edu/c.php?g=448703&p=10035852.
  Took: Latin ring inscription, central device, founding year in the ring.
- UNC seal history, https://blogs.lib.unc.edu/hill/2016/05/12/the-history-of-the-university-seal/.
  Took: seals simplify over time; a plain device in a double ring reads as
  more official than an elaborate one.
- "Sign Languages", The Beauty of Transport, https://thebeautyoftransport.com/2014/12/03/sign-languages-transport-and-ministry-typefaces-uk/.
  Took: the "bowler-hatted civil servant" tone, capital headings, monochrome.
- Roman lettering, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_lettering.
  Took: spaced Roman capitals, centred layouts, Trajan-derived faces for the ring.
- Müller-Brockmann posters, https://ilovetypography.com/2013/01/12/a-firm-turn-toward-the-objective-josef-muller-brockmann-1948-1981/.
  Took: strict column grid, wide margins, one very large element (the URL)
  against small quiet text.

## Palette

Paper `#F4F1E8`, ink `#1B1A17`, rule `#8C877B`, faint rule `#D6D1C4`,
rank-one green `#245A3C`. No red, no MIT grey. Green is ledger ink and belongs
to nothing at MIT.

## Type

Cinzel (Google Fonts) for the seal ring, house name, and spaced-capital labels;
fallback Georgia. EB Garamond (Google Fonts) for everything else, with tabular
figures; fallback Georgia, Times New Roman, serif.

## Why it suits a dorm ladder

The ladder's real product is trust in a number nobody enforces. Deadpan
officialdom makes that number feel binding without any authority behind it,
and residents already read the notice board with exactly the half-attention
this sheet is written for.
