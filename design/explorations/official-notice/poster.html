The House has always had an official rating.
It is now written down.

ashdown.win is the table tennis ladder of Ashdown House: one table, one board, one rating per resident. No account is required. Any resident may enter a player and record any match, and the board updates at once.

PROCEDURE

  1. Create your name. Enter your full name once. It is the only thing the House records about you.
  2. Record a match. Choose the two players, or four for doubles, and tap the side that won. Either player may file it.
  3. Watch the board. Ratings are recomputed from the full match record on every view. Nothing is stored; nothing is hidden.

RATING RULE

§ 4. After each match, R′ = R + K (S − E), where E = 1 / (1 + 10^((Ropp − R) / 400)); S is 1 for a win and 0 for a loss; K = 32 for singles and 16 for doubles; every player begins at 1000.

REGULATIONS

  1. A player is a name and nothing more. The Committee does not collect contact details and will not forward messages.
  2. A doubles pair plays as the mean of its two ratings. Both partners move by the same amount at half the weight of singles.
  3. A match filed in error may be deleted by anyone. The deletion is entered in the record beside the match it voids.
  4. The record is public in full. Cheating is possible and would be visible; in a house of a few hundred people this is considered sufficient.
  5. The player standing first on the board is shown in green. No other honour attaches to the position.
  6. The table remains the property of the House and may be used without recording a match.
