ashdown.win is the public Elo ladder for the Ashdown table. No accounts, no app to install: one rating per player, recomputed from every match ever recorded, and every match is visible to everyone.

1

Create your name

Once, on your phone. Full name by default, or your MIT username if you'd rather. You appear on the board at 1000 straight away.

2

Record a match

Type both names, tap the winner, done. Flip the doubles switch for two per side. Anyone can record for anyone; nothing is hidden.

3

Watch the board

The winner takes points from the loser the moment it's saved. Your page shows your rating climbing or sliding, and the games that did it.

The rule
R′ = R + K·(S − E)   where  E = 1 / (1 + 10(Ropp − R)/400)
K = 32 singles · K = 16 doubles · S = 1 win, 0 loss · everyone starts at 1000 · a doubles team plays as the average of its two ratings. Same Elo as chess, nothing tuned.
