Round 3 — eight contained pills

Everything here is a pill or capsule with a dot and a label. Nothing floats on the blue, so every option reads as a control you can press rather than as decoration — that was the fault in round 2's bare ring, and it is gone.

The dot pulses only while a match is under ten minutes old. After that it goes solid, then calm, then grey. Colour is restricted to the brand pink and three reds pushed away from it — pink #fb3aa3, REC red #e8232a, crimson #b8102e, vermilion #f4501e — plus the two treatments where the red lives only in the dot and the pill itself is ink or white.

Every option shows its whole life cycle in the real top bar: under ten minutes with the pulse running, ten to sixty minutes, a few hours, nothing today, and the live state again under reduced motion.

The width budget, still binding. At 390 px the top bar has 354 px of usable width and the current contents already spend 355.9. The indicator takes the "Log" chip's slot, leaving about 48 px. That is roughly a dot plus three characters — which is the whole reason options 7 and 8 below have to buy their space from the logo. Each option is measured at runtime across all four states and reports its widest.

?Pink or red?

The indicator lands eight pixels from the logo, so the hue has to be judged against that pink and not on its own. Below, one identical pill in four fills, then the same information with the colour confined to the dot. Same label, same shape, same dot — only the colour treatment changes.

Every state is legible from fill, dot and label with all motion stopped, so prefers-reduced-motion: reduce freezes each live state in its emphasised pose rather than blanking it — the fifth mock in every option is that frozen rendering, and the toggle at the top applies it to the whole page.
