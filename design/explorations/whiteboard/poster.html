Ashdown House · MIT

Who is actually the best
at ping pong in this house?

Now there is a board that answers it. ashdown.win is a public Elo ladder for the Ashdown table. No account, no app, no login: write your name once, and any match you play moves your number.

the winner takes points off the loser.
every match. every number is public.

How it works, in three steps

1
Create your name. Once. Full name, or your MIT username. That is the whole sign-up.
2
Record a match. Two names, tap the winner, done. Doubles works too. Anyone at the table can file it.
3
Watch the board. Ratings update immediately. Your page shows a graph and every match you have played.
this is the whole algorithm. really.
R′ = R + K·(S − E)   with  E = 1 / (1 + 10(Ropp − R) / 400)
S is 1 for a win, 0 for a loss. K = 32 singles, K = 16 doubles. Everyone starts at 1000. Same Elo that chess uses, nothing hidden.
