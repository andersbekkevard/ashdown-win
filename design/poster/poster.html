Who rulesthe table?

A public Elo ladder for the ping-pong table downstairs. No accounts, no app, no excuses. Play, tap in the result, and watch your name climb the board.

1

Create your name

Once. Full name, MIT username, or anything the house will recognise. You start at 1000.

2

Record a match

Two names, tap the winner, done. Doubles works too. Anyone can file it, and every match is public.

3

Watch the board

The ladder updates the moment you submit. Winner takes points, loser gives them up. First place wears the crown.
